Tabering Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Tabering (the “App”) handles information about its users.

1. Operator

ほくほく文庫 (Hokuhoku Bunko, an independent developer)
The operator’s name and address will be provided without delay to anyone who contacts us.

2. Information We Collect

  • Email address (when you register an account)
  • Username and profile picture
  • Places you add, visit records, comments and ratings
  • Photos attached to visit records (taken or chosen by the user)
  • Location information (when showing the map and when searching for and suggesting nearby places; requires your device’s permission)
  • Group information and group membership status
  • Device identifiers and other information used to deliver ads (collected by Google AdMob; see “8. Advertising and Tracking” for details)

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide the App’s features (managing places, visit records, photos and group sharing)
  • To respond to reports of inappropriate photos
  • To provide user support
  • To improve the App and fix problems

4. Sharing with Third Parties

The App uses the following services.

  • Supabase (database, authentication and photo storage): Privacy Policy
  • Google Maps Platform (showing the map, searching for places and showing place photos): Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob (delivering ads): Privacy Policy

We do not provide personal information to any third parties other than those listed above.

5. Data Storage and Deletion

The information we collect is stored on Supabase’s servers. When you delete your account, all related data (places you added, visit records, photos, reviews and group memberships) is deleted. Groups you created are handed over to the member who joined earliest, if there are other members (otherwise they are deleted). Visit records that other users made at places you added keep only that place’s name and location.

6. Photos

Photos attached to visit records are resized and stored in Supabase storage (private), and can be viewed only by you and members of the groups you shared that record with. If you don’t share a record with any group, its photos are shown only to you. When resizing, we remove additional information from photos, such as where they were taken. You can delete photos at any time from the photo screen in the App, and when you delete a record or your account, its photos are also deleted.

You can report other users’ photos from the photo screen. Reported photos are immediately hidden from other users; the operator views them for review and deletes those that violate the Terms of Use. You can also choose not to see photos from specific users.

For places without your own photos, we may show a Google Maps photo of the place along with the photographer’s name. To get these photos, we send the place’s ID (or, for places without a known ID, the place name and location) to Google.

7. Location Information

Location information is obtained according to your device’s permission settings and is used only to show the map and to search for and suggest nearby places. For searches and suggestions, the location at the center of the map is sent to Google (Google Maps Platform). It is not sent to or stored on the App’s servers. You can restrict the use of location information in your device settings.

8. Advertising and Tracking

On the free plan, the App shows ads from Google AdMob. To deliver ads, Google may collect device identifiers and information about your network connection.

If you allow tracking when your iPhone asks, you may see ads tailored to your interests based on your activity in other apps and websites. If you don’t allow it, ads are still shown, but your device’s advertising identifier is not used. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking on your iPhone.

In Europe (EEA, UK and Switzerland), the App shows a screen asking for your consent for advertising. You can change your consent at any time from “Change Ad Consent” on the Profile tab in the App.

No ads are shown on the Premium plan. The App does not use any analytics tools. For how information is handled in ads, please see Google’s policies.

9. Age Restrictions

The App is intended for ages 4 and up.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy as needed. If there are important changes, we will let you know in the App.
