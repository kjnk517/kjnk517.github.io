Nagaya Share

Nagaya Share is an iPhone app where, as you log the meals you cook, the scent draws families of little animals to move into your row house. Your food photos turn into small pixel-art dishes that appear on your residents’ tables. Even without a photo, you can pick a dish from the list and its pixel art will be served. You can also log meals you ate out or bought.

FAQ

If I get a new phone, can I carry my town over?
If you move your whole iPhone using an iCloud backup or Quick Start, your town and records come along with it. There is no way to move just the app to another device.
How do I restore my purchase?
In Settings, under “Expand the Town,” tap “Restore Purchase.” You need to be signed in with the Apple ID you used to make the purchase.
How do I stop the full-screen ads?
Buying the one-time “Expand the Town” purchase removes the full-screen ads. You can also use the scent refill and swap requests on the notice board without watching ads. Buy it once and it’s yours for good (it is not a monthly subscription).
My residents always say the same thing.
Each resident’s line is picked from a set of prepared phrases to match the time of day, the season, the weather, and the dish you cooked that day. We try to avoid repeats, but in the same situation you may see similar lines.
The genre picked from my photo is wrong.
You can change the genre at any time while logging. After you’ve logged an entry, open it in the Meal Journal and choose the genre again with “Edit.” If you fix it on the same day you logged it, the scent changes too.
My residents are missing.
If you haven’t logged anything for a while, residents go off on a trip. They haven’t gone for good. Log just one meal and they’ll come back.
I can’t build a fifth house.
You can build up to four houses. From the fifth family on, they wait to move in. Buying the one-time “Expand the Town” purchase removes the limit, and they’ll move in right away.
The widget picture isn’t changing.
Widgets are redrawn at intervals decided by iOS. If it still hasn’t changed after a while, remove the widget and add it again.
Whose weather does the app show?
It shows the weather, plus sunrise and sunset times, for the town where the prefectural government of your chosen Japanese prefecture is located. The app doesn’t use your location, so even within the same prefecture it may differ a little from the sky over your own town. You can change your region at any time with the region tag at the top of the town screen. If you haven’t chosen one, you’ll see the sky over Tokyo.
On an iPhone set to a region outside Japan, the sky and weather come from a major city in the world that matches your device’s time zone. You can choose a different city with the same tag. The time of day in your town always follows your device’s clock.
How do I get keepsakes and decorations?
You can earn them by cooking a house’s signature dish several times, by growing closer to that family, by talking to a resident for several days, and so on. Winter keepsakes are earned by the number of days you log during the winter holiday season (December 21 to January 8), and you can check your count on the Stamp Card. Hints on how to get each item are in the Keepsake Book. If you miss a winter keepsake, you can earn it again next winter.
What happens if I delete the app?
Your town, records, and photos are all deleted. If you have an iCloud backup, you can restore them from it.
Can I use images I found online, or photos taken by someone else?
Please see “About Photos” below.
I found a line or a picture that bothers me.
Sorry for the trouble. Please let us know at the contact address below, and tell us which screen you saw it on.

About Photos

Please use photos of food that you took yourself. If you publish pixel art or share cards made from photos taken by other people or images found online, you may infringe the copyright of the original photos.

Photos and pixel art are handled only inside this iPhone. The app does not send or publish them anywhere (if you use iCloud Backup, they are included in your own backup). They leave your device only when you share a share card yourself. If you post something made from a photo you didn’t take yourself to social media or elsewhere, please make sure you’re allowed to use the photo, and do so at your own responsibility.

Privacy Policy

This app, “Nagaya Share” (the “App”), respects the privacy of its users, and handles information as set out below.

Information the Developer Collects

The developer does not collect any information that identifies you, such as your name or email address. The App has no account registration or login. The developer does not operate a server and does not use any service that analyzes how the App is used.

What Is Stored on Your Device

Your food photos and cutouts, pixel-art dishes, records (date, time of day, genre, ingredients, dish name), the state of your residents and town, and your settings are stored only on your iPhone and are never sent to the developer. If you use iCloud Backup, they are included in your own backup. They are deleted when you delete the App.

Camera and Photos

The camera is used only when you photograph a meal. The App loads only the photos you choose in the system photo picker, and does not access your entire photo library. Photos and pixel art are processed only on your device, and location data contained in a photo is removed when it is saved. A share card leaves your device only when you share it yourself. The App saves to the Photos app only when you choose to.

Location and Weather

The App does not use your device’s location and does not ask for location permission. You simply choose your region from the 47 Japanese prefectures. On an iPhone set to a region outside Japan, the App picks one major city in the world based on your device’s time zone setting (you can also choose a different one). The time zone setting is only read on your device and is not sent anywhere.

To match the sky in your town to the actual weather, the App sends only the coordinates of the prefectural government of your chosen prefecture, or the coordinates of your chosen city (both are fixed values built into the App), to Apple through iOS WeatherKit, and receives a weather forecast. The developer does not receive these coordinates. Apple’s handling of this data follows the Apple Privacy Policy.

Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob, an ad serving service of Google LLC, to show full-screen ads and optional video ads (rewarded ads). Rewarded ads play only when you tap the button: when you want to add a little more scent after logging, and when you swap a notice board request from the second time on. If you watch to the end, the reward is applied to your town. To serve ads and measure their performance, Google and its partners may collect and use your advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking), your IP address and the approximate region inferred from it, your device model and OS version, and records of ad impressions and taps. Google receives this information directly; the developer does not.

For details, please see the Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses data when you use our partners’ sites or apps, and Google’s advertising policies.

Before any ad is shown, the App asks once, in the iOS “Allow Tracking” prompt, whether you allow tracking with the advertising identifier. This prompt appears when you first launch the App. No ads are loaded until you answer. If you decline, ads just won’t be personalized to you, and every feature of the App remains available. You can change this setting at any time in the iOS Settings app under Privacy & Security > Tracking. If the laws of your region require consent for advertising, the App shows Google’s consent screen.

The “Expand the Town” One-Time Purchase and Ads

If you buy the one-time “Expand the Town” purchase, full-screen ads are no longer shown, and you can use the scent refill and swap notice board requests without watching ads. After the purchase, the App neither loads ads nor asks for tracking permission.

Purchases are processed through Apple’s App Store, and the developer does not receive your payment information.

Notifications

The App’s notifications are only those scheduled and shown on your device. They are not sent from a server.

Contact Emails

If you send us an email, your email address and the contents of your message are used only to reply and to investigate problems.

Children’s Privacy

The developer does not collect information from users, regardless of age. The information Google collects through ad serving is as described above under “Advertising.”

Disclosure to Third Parties

Because the developer holds no information about users, the developer does not provide any to third parties. The information Google collects directly through ad serving is as described above under “Advertising.”
