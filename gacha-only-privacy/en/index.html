Fictional Gacha

"Fictional Gacha" is a joke app where you pull the gacha of "Starseer Chronicle", a mobile game that does not exist. There is no main story. All characters, the world, and the stories are fictional.

Support

For questions about this app, please contact support@hokuhokusan.com.

Privacy Policy

"Fictional Gacha" (the "App") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting personal information.

Information We Collect

The App does not collect personal information that identifies you (such as your name or email address). There is no account registration or login.

Your gacha results, owned characters, Starstone balance, and the record of side stories you have read are stored on your device. They are deleted when you delete the App.

To improve the App, it sends the following usage information and stores it with Supabase, a data storage service:

  • When you finish or skip a side story or event story, which story you read and how far you read (the story number, the number of lines read, whether you read to the end or skipped, and whether it was your first time)
  • The number of the character or scene illustration you chose for the app icon or the title screen (when you choose it, and once for each app version)

Each is sent with the app version and the display language. This information does not include anything that can identify you or your device (such as the advertising identifier, device identifiers, or your save data). If sending fails, the App works as usual.

The App does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts, or health information.

No In-App Purchases

The App has no in-app purchases. Starstones, the in-game currency, increase only over time and through login bonuses, watching ads, and finishing side stories. You cannot buy Starstones with real money.

Advertising

The App shows ads by Google AdMob (banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads). To deliver ads, Google and its partners may collect information such as the advertising identifier (IDFA). For details, please see Google's Privacy Policy.

For tracking used to personalize ads, the App asks for your permission on the iOS "Allow Tracking" screen. If you do not allow it, ads are simply not personalized and the App's features are not limited. You can change this setting at any time in the iOS Settings app.

Sharing with Third Parties

We do not provide collected information to third parties except as required by law or as necessary for the purposes described above.

Contact

For questions about this app, please contact support@hokuhokusan.com.

Changes to This Policy

We may change this policy as needed. The updated policy will be posted on this page.
