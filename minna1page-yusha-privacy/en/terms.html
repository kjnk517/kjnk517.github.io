Everyone's One Page: Hero Edition Terms of Use

These terms set out the conditions for using Everyone's One Page: Hero Edition (the "App"). By using the App, you are deemed to have agreed to these terms.

1. The Service

The App is for reading a manga series that moves forward one page a day. The next page's development is decided by readers' votes: of the three posts that arrive each day, the one with the most votes wins. When votes are tied, or when no votes are cast, the winner is decided by lottery.

The post cards and the manga are created by generative AI. The App has no way to write text.

"Everyone's Series" is a single series shared by all users. If you start reading partway through, you join partway through, and the earlier pages can be read from "Pages so far." When a story ends, its final page is shown that day, and a new series starts the next day.

2. Fees

Reading and voting on "Everyone's Series" are free.

The paid plan (Pro) is an auto-renewing subscription at 320 yen per month (tax included; in other countries and regions, the price shown in the App Store). With Pro, you can generate one page of your own series every day, and no ads are shown. If you continue your own series for free, you can draw one page a day for the first three days, and then up to one page every 7 days.

Payment is made through Apple's App Store. The subscription renews automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can cancel at any time from the subscription management screen in the iOS Settings app. If you cancel partway through a period, you can keep using Pro until the end of that period, but there are no prorated refunds. Whether a refund is possible follows Apple's rules.
