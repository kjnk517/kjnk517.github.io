Everyone's One Page: Hero Edition

Everyone's One Page: Hero Edition is a manga series that moves forward one page a day. What happens on the next page is decided by readers' votes: of the three posts that arrive each day, the one with the most votes wins. The posts and the manga are generated by AI. When a story ends, a new series starts the next day.

The app has no way to write text. The only things users do are vote on choices and read.

Terms of Use

Privacy Policy

Everyone's One Page: Hero Edition (the "App") respects your privacy and works to protect your personal information.

Information We Collect

The App does not collect information that identifies you (such as your name, email address, phone number, or date of birth). There is no account registration or login.

On first launch, the App generates one random identifier on your device. It is used only to prevent duplicate votes, to manage the daily generation limit, and to count at which step people stop using the App. It is not Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) or a hardware identifier, and it cannot be matched with other apps or other companies' services. Deleting the App erases this identifier from your device.

Together with this identifier, the App's server stores the following:

  • Which post you voted for, and the date
  • Daily usage counts, to manage the generation limit
  • Operation logs (processing time, whether it succeeded, and the cost of generation)
  • Records of how far you have gone in the App (the type and date of steps such as opening the App, flipping a card, or opening the vote screen; your choices and any text are not included)

Manga pages and images generated in "My Series," your notification time, and other settings are stored only on your device and are never sent to the App's server. Deleting the App erases them, and they cannot be transferred to a new device.

The App does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, or health information.

Use of Generative AI

The post cards and manga shown in the App are created using generative AI from Anthropic and OpenAI. Because the App has no text input, nothing you write is ever sent to these companies. What is sent is only the story's summary so far and the content of the chosen post.

Generated content is created automatically, and we do not guarantee its accuracy or that it will suit everyone. If you see problematic content, please contact us at the address above.

Where Information Is Stored

The App's server runs on Supabase's service, and information may be stored on servers outside Japan.

Advertising

The App shows ads from Google AdMob. To deliver ads, Google and its partners may collect information such as the advertising identifier (IDFA). For details, please see Google's Privacy Policy.

Tracking used to personalize ads is requested through the iOS "Allow Tracking" prompt. If you do not allow it, ads simply will not be personalized; the App's features are not limited. You can change this setting at any time in the iOS Settings app.

In the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, before showing ads, the App asks through Google's consent form whether you agree to the use of information for advertising. You can change your choice at any time from "Change ad consent" in the App's Settings.

If you use the paid plan (Pro), no ads are shown.

Purchases

The App's paid plan (Pro) is an auto-renewing subscription through Apple's App Store. Payments are processed by Apple, and the App never receives payment information such as credit card numbers.

Purchase status is checked by verifying Apple's signed purchase record on the App's server. No other company's service is used to check purchases. The server stores only the random identifier above and the expiration date of Pro.

Notifications

The App's notifications are created and shown on your device; they are not sent from a server. If you do not allow notifications, the App's features are not limited.

Sharing with Third Parties

We do not provide collected information to third parties, except as required by law or as necessary for the purposes described above.

Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Deleting Your Information

Deleting the App erases all information stored on your device. If you would like the votes and operation logs on the server deleted, please contact us.
